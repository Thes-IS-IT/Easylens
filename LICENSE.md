## Assistive Technology & Safety Disclaimer

> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:** EasyLens is an experimental assistive aid designed to augment spatial awareness. It is **not** a medical device, a replacement for a white cane, a certified guide dog, or human orientation and mobility assistance. Users must exercise caution, remain aware of ambient environmental auditory cues, and adhere to local pedestrian safety standards at all times. The authors and Holy Angel University assume no legal liability for physical injuries, property damage, or navigational errors incurred while operating the system.
